Three ways to participate

Own the asset — or lend against it

Not everyone wants to be an owner. Some investors just want a fixed, predictable premium without the risk and upside of equity. You choose your seat in the capital stack.

Equity · Control

Common A

The control class. Voting rights on major decisions, sponsor-aligned, and full exposure to cash flow and appreciation after other classes are paid.

RoleOwner
VotingYes
ReturnResidual cash flow + appreciation
RiskHighest / Highest upside
Equity · Passive

Common B

Passive ownership. A preferred return paid before Common A shares in the residual, then pro-rata participation in cash flow and appreciation. No management duties.

RoleOwner (passive)
VotingNon-voting
ReturnPreferred return + pro-rata upside
RiskModerate
Debt · Lender

Investor Note

You are a lender, not an owner. Your capital is a note secured against the project that pays a fixed premium and is repaid at term. Paid before any equity distribution — no ownership, no voting, no exposure to the downside of equity.

RoleLender (creditor)
VotingNone
ReturnFixed premium (e.g. 9%/yr)
PriorityPaid before equity
