The Model

From property to portfolio, funded together

Our platform continuously screens the multi-residential market, packages the strongest opportunities into a digital prospectus, and lets you choose exactly how you want to participate — as an owner or as a lender.

1

We source & screen

Every day the platform scans multi-residential listings across our target markets and ranks them against strict cash-flow and value-add criteria. Only the deals that pencil out move forward.

2

We build the prospectus

Each shortlisted property gets a full digital prospectus — underwriting, pro-forma financials, use of funds, projected returns, and risk factors — assembled online and shared with prospective investors.

3

You choose your role

Invest as a Common A or Common B shareholder to own a piece of the asset, or participate as a secured lender — not an owner — who earns a fixed premium on the money you put in.

4

We operate & distribute

We close, stabilize, and professionally manage the property. Shareholders and lenders receive scheduled distributions and quarterly financial statements through the investor portal.

Two Ways to Participate

Own the asset, or lend against it

Pick the structure that matches your appetite for risk and return. Equity shares in the upside; the secured note trades upside for a fixed, predictable premium.
