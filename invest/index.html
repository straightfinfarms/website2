⚖️ Compliance note

Raising money from investors — as equity or as debt — is a securities activity. In the US this typically means an exemption (Reg CF, Reg D 506(b)/(c), or Reg A+), KYC/AML, accredited-investor verification, escrow, and often a registered funding portal or broker-dealer. This tool models and documents deals; the actual raise must be run through a compliant path with securities counsel.

Select or create a syndication to see its capital stack, cap table, per-investor returns, and prospectus.
